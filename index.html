ABOUT ME

A developing technical practitioner and aspiring founder.

I'm Mayur Pisal, a student and independent builder with a deep interest in cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and software development. I spend my time exploring Linux, networking, cloud technology, and automation — always looking for ways to turn ideas into practical, working projects.

My journey is driven by curiosity. I learn best by building, breaking, and understanding how systems work under the hood. Whether it's a small web app, a security experiment, or a personal tool, I focus on making things that are functional and educational.
